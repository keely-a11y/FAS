Studio

Ferrier Architecture Studio is a Los Angeles–based practice founded in 2010 by Graham Ferrier and Keely Colcleugh. We work across residential, multi-family, and mixed-use typologies — from custom single-family homes to larger urban infill developments.

Our approach is rooted in an understanding of site, climate, and the life a building will accommodate. We use advanced BIM and visualisation tools to develop design intent early and with precision, reducing uncertainty for clients and construction teams alike.
