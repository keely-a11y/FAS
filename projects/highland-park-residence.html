Residence

← All Projects
Type Single Family Residential
Location Highland Park, Los Angeles
Area 3,200 sf
Status Completed 2024
Description

A hillside residence that negotiates a steep slope through a series of offset volumes, each capturing distinct views of the San Gabriel Mountains and the city below. The plan is organised along a single north–south spine, allowing rooms to step down the site in response to topography.

Exposed board-formed concrete, raw steel, and white plaster reflect the light and materiality of Northeast Los Angeles. Deep overhangs provide passive shading while framing views as a series of composed vignettes.
