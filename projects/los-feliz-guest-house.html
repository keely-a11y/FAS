Type ADU / Guest House
Location Los Feliz, Los Angeles
Area 650 sf
Status Completed 2022
Description

A compact guest house and accessory dwelling unit on an existing residential lot in Los Feliz, maximising habitable area within California's ADU regulations while maintaining privacy from the primary residence.

The unit is positioned at the rear of the site against the property boundary, with a single-pitch roof plane that funnels natural light deep into the open-plan living space. A small private courtyard mediates between the ADU and the main dwelling, planted with drought-tolerant species.
