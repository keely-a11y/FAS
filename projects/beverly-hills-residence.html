Beverly Hills
Residence

← All Projects
Type Single Family Residential
Location Beverly Hills, CA
Area 6,200 sf
Status Completed 2023
Description

A 6,200 sf private residence on a sloped hillside lot, organised around a central courtyard that mediates between the street entry and the canyon views beyond. The plan unfolds as a series of interlocking volumes that step down the topography, each shift in level marking a transition between public and private zones of the house.

The courtyard serves as the social heart of the home — a sheltered exterior room that connects living areas, the pool terrace, and the primary suite loggia. Materials include board-formed concrete, hand-trowelled plaster, and rift-sawn white oak, chosen for their capacity to age gracefully in the Southern California climate.
