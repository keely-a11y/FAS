Type Multi-Family Residential
Location Silver Lake, Los Angeles
Units 12 Apartments
Status Completed 2023
Description

Twelve apartment units organised around a shared courtyard, providing passive cooling and a communal threshold between street and dwelling in a dense urban neighbourhood.

The building is arranged in two parallel bars connected by an open breezeway. Unit plans are deep and narrow, with cross-ventilation enabled by operable glazing at front and rear. Shared laundry, bike storage, and landscaped seating occupy the ground level courtyard.
