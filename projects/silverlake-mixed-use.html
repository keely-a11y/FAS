Type Mixed-Use
Location Silver Lake, Los Angeles
Area 6,200 sf
Status Completed 2021
Description

Street-level café and retail beneath four apartments — a model for gentle densification in an established neighbourhood. The commercial tenancies are designed as a single flexible space that can be subdivided, with folding glazed facades that open fully to the sidewalk in warm weather.

The four apartments above each have distinct layouts responding to orientation — northern units are deeper with skylights, southern units have narrower plans with full-height south-facing glazing and external louvres for solar control.
