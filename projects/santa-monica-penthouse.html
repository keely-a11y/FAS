← All Projects
Type Residential Renovation
Location Santa Monica, CA
Area 2,800 sf
Status Completed 2020
Description

A full renovation of an existing penthouse that opens the plan to Pacific Ocean views through a new roof terrace and floor-to-ceiling glazing on the western elevation. Interior walls were removed to create a single open living, dining, and kitchen space oriented toward the horizon.

Materials are deliberately restrained — bleached white oak, honed limestone, and plaster — so that the shifting ocean light and colour remain the primary visual experience. A new steel and glass stair connects the main level to the private roof terrace above.
