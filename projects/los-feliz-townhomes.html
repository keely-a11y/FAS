Units 6 Townhomes
Status Completed 2021
Description

Six townhome units designed as a series of stacked volumes, each with a private outdoor terrace and views toward the Hollywood Hills. The units are organised in two rows of three, separated by a landscaped pedestrian lane that serves as shared outdoor space and a buffer from the street.

Each townhome has its own front door directly off the lane, providing a sense of individual address within the density of a multi-family project. Ground level bedrooms open to private patios, while upper living areas capture views through full-height glazing.
