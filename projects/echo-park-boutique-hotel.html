Echo Park
Boutique Hotel

← All Projects
Type Boutique Hotel
Location Echo Park, Los Angeles
Rooms 24 Keys
Status Completed 2022
Description

A 24-room boutique hotel responding to Echo Park's creative culture through textured board-formed concrete, locally sourced timber, and a rooftop terrace overlooking the reservoir. The building steps back from the street at each level, creating generous planted terraces that soften the urban edge.

Rooms are arranged in a pinwheel plan around a central light well that acts as a social hub for guests. The ground level opens fully to the street with a café and small event space, activating the corner site throughout the day.
