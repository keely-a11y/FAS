# How to update the HKLA website

Everything on the site is edited through structured fields. You change words and images; the design takes care of itself. There is no way to break the layout, colors, or typography, so edit with confidence.

## Add a project

1. In the dashboard, go to **Projects, Add New**.
2. **Title**: the project name, for example "Wilmington Greenbelt".
3. **Story headline**: the one-line story of the place, under 10 words. This is the most important field on the page. Lead with the story, not the specs.
4. **Story intro**: 2 to 3 short paragraphs that open the narrative.
5. **Hero image**: the strongest photo of the finished place, at least 2400px wide.
6. **Facts**: client, location, size, completion, services. Leave blank anything that does not apply; blank facts simply do not appear.
7. **Story sections**: build the narrative in order with the "Add section" button. Alternate text with imagery. Use a **Sketch spotlight** where a hand drawing tells the story best; it gets a special treatment on the page.
8. **Sector**: tick one sector in the right-hand panel. This drives the filter on the Projects page.
9. **Featured image** (right-hand panel): usually the same as the hero image. This is what appears on project cards.
10. Click **Publish**. Check the live page.

## Edit a team bio

Go to **People**, open the person, edit their role, credentials, bio, or headshot, and click **Update**. Headshots should be square, at least 800px. Drag people in the People list to reorder them on the site.

## Post an open role

Go to **Site Settings, Open roles**, click **Add role**, fill in the title, type, location, description, and an apply link (a normal URL or `mailto:careers@hklainc.com`). Remove a role by hovering over its row and clicking the minus.

## Update contact details

**Site Settings** holds the address, phone, email, social links, and the footer line.

## Journal

**Journal** entries use a simple editor limited to text and images. Write short and confident, like everything else on the site.

## Two rules that matter

- **Every image needs alt text**: a short description of what is in the picture, written when you upload it. The site flags images that are missing it. This keeps the site accessible, which our public agency clients require.
- **Image sizes**: heroes at least 2400px wide, story images at least 1600px, headshots 800px square. Bigger is fine; the site resizes automatically.

## Writing in the HKLA voice

Short declarative sentences. No jargon, no hedging. Instead of "Several existing elements were identified which could have been neglected," write "A struggling tree, overhead flight paths, chain-link fencing. Where others saw problems, we saw potential." Use periods, commas, or colons. No em dashes.
